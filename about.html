About BudgetTools

Simple financial planning tools that help you make better decisions with your money.

What We Do

BudgetTools is an online financial planning platform that provides two things:

  • 10 free calculators — instant answers for wedding budgets, event break-even analysis, freelancer take-home pay, rental yield, loan affordability, and more. No account required, no data stored.
  • 5 premium planning templates — full-featured tools for wedding planning, event budgeting, travel planning, cafe costing, and inventory management. Premium templates include cloud sync, unlimited entries, and data export.

Business Category

BudgetTools operates in the financial technology (fintech) and online software-as-a-service (SaaS) space. We provide digital financial planning tools delivered entirely through a web browser. No physical goods are sold or shipped.

How It Works

All calculators are free to use without an account. Premium templates can be explored in demo mode for free. When you're ready to save your own data and access Pro features, you can subscribe to a monthly plan. Subscriptions are processed securely through Cashfree and can be cancelled at any time from your account page.
