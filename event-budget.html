The Problem

Why Event Budgets Fail

Research from Eventbrite, Cvent, and industry surveys consistently shows the same patterns — and they're all preventable.

receipt

Hidden Fees Kill Margins

Venue service charges (18–25%), catering gratuities, overtime labor, and permit fees are rarely in the original quote. They add 30–40% to your baseline cost.

show_chart

No Real-Time P&L Visibility

Most planners use spreadsheets updated manually once a week. By the time they realize they're over budget, it's too late to reduce costs or sell more tickets.

group_off

Break-Even Blindspot

Organisers set ticket prices without knowing how many attendees they need to break even. They often under-price tickets and over-deliver on attendee experience — at a loss.
