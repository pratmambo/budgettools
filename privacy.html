Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026

1. Who We Are

BudgetTools (budgettemplates.shop) is operated by Juturu Preetam, a sole proprietorship based in India. When this policy says "we", "us", or "our", it refers to BudgetTools.

Contact: support.budgettemplates@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

Free calculators (no account required)

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data when you use our free calculators. All calculations happen in your browser. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies on calculator pages.

Account registration

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Full name (if provided)
  • Password (stored as a hash — we never see or store your plaintext password)
  • Profile picture URL (if you sign in with Google)

Premium template usage

When you use a premium template, we store the data you enter (budget items, guest lists, inventory entries, etc.) in our database so it can sync across your devices. This data is linked to your account and protected by row-level security — no other user can see it.

Payment information

Payments are processed entirely by Razorpay. We never receive, see, or store your card number, UPI ID, or bank account details. Razorpay provides us with a subscription ID and payment status, which we store to manage your access.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain your account and subscriptions
  • To sync your template data across your devices
  • To send you transactional emails (account confirmation, password resets, payment receipts via Razorpay)
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not send marketing emails.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate BudgetTools:

Supabase (Authentication & Database)

Stores your account credentials and template data. Servers located in the region selected during project setup. Supabase Privacy Policy

Razorpay (Payments)

Processes subscription payments. Handles all sensitive payment data (card numbers, UPI, net banking credentials). PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant. Razorpay Privacy Policy

Netlify (Hosting)

Hosts our website and serverless functions. May collect basic access logs (IP address, request URL, timestamp). Netlify Privacy Policy

Google (OAuth sign-in, optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google. We do not access any other Google account data. Google Privacy Policy

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use:

  • localStorage — to cache your template data locally for fast loading. This stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
  • sessionStorage — to manage the 10-minute demo trial timer. Cleared when you close the browser tab.
  • Supabase auth tokens — stored in localStorage to keep you signed in between visits. These are JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) and expire automatically.

6. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption). Your template data is protected by Supabase's row-level security policies, ensuring only you can access your own data. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Our serverless functions use a service-role key that is never exposed to the browser.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data and template data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we will delete all associated data from our database. Razorpay may retain payment records independently as required by financial regulations.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — view all your template data from within the app
  • Correct your data — edit your templates at any time
  • Delete your data — delete your account from the account page, or email us
  • Cancel your subscription — cancel at any time from your account page

To exercise any of these rights, email support.budgettemplates@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

BudgetTools is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of BudgetTools after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
