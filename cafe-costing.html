FAQ

Common Questions

Industry benchmark is 28–32% for food, and 20–25% for beverages. Coffee typically has the lowest food cost (15–22%), making it the highest GP item in most cafes. If you're above 35%, you likely need to re-price or find cheaper suppliers.

Food cost % only covers ingredient costs. Labor (typically 28–35% of revenue) is tracked separately. Your "prime cost" (food + labor) should ideally be under 60–65% of revenue. We show this in the break-even tab alongside your fixed costs.

In the free version, update ingredient costs manually and see item costs recalculate instantly. The Pro version (coming soon) includes supplier price sync and change alerts.

Enter your fixed costs (rent, utilities, subscriptions) and variable costs. We calculate the minimum covers (customers served) per day needed to cover all costs at your average spend per cover.

Menu engineering classifies items as: Stars (high margin, popular — promote these), Plough Horses (popular but low margin — re-price or find cheaper ingredients), Puzzles (high margin, low popularity — promote more), Dogs (low margin, low popularity — consider removing).
